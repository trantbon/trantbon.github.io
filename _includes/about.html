**Last updated: **September 2026** My own quote: "I write my legacy in sweat and ink—one stride, one study, one dream at a time."

I am a highly driven and versatile individual, excelling in both academics and athletics. With a track record of excellence, I've earned numerous trophies, medals, and awards that speak to my dedication across disciplines. As an all-rounder, I take pride in balancing rigorous academic pursuits with elite-level performance on the field. Among the many sports I compete in, soccer stands out as a passion and a strength—one in which I've consistently dominated, collecting a wealth of accolades and team triumphs along the way. My journey is fueled by discipline, passion, and an unrelenting pursuit of excellence.

My success in academics and sport is fueled not just by talent, but by the habits I’ve built beyond them. Chess sharpens my strategic thinking and quick decision-making, while swimming strengthens my physical endurance. These aren't just hobbies—they're integral to how I train, think, and perform. I rely on a strict daily routine to maintain that edge, because when I slip, fatigue takes over. Discipline isn’t optional—it’s my foundation.

Over time, I’ve discovered a deep passion not only for sports 🏀🏊 but also for creative writing ✍️. I love letting my imagination run wild—crafting stories and exploring ideas without the fear of being judged for how “crazy” or out-of-the-box they might be 🚀. Writing gives me the freedom to express myself in ways that feel truly authentic.

I’ve also found myself developing a growing appreciation for mathematics ➕➗. It's like a fascinating puzzle that challenges and excites me with every problem I solve. I’ve been putting a lot of effort into preparing for a major academic milestone—the Selective School Test (or SS for short) 🎯📚. Even though I failed because of unfair conditions and my past mistakes, I am looking forward to learn from my past deeds and see how I progress from this major setback while looking for major opportunities I can take advantage of.
